# Cookie Policy - SeaHype Marine Biology Education

**Effective date:** 5 July 2026

## Short version
The App does not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics.

## Local storage vs cookies
To remember your progress, settings, and display name, the App uses your browser's
local storage on your own device. Local storage is not a cookie and is not used to
track you across sites or sessions on other services. It never leaves your device and
is not transmitted to us or any third party.

## Third-party cookies
The App bundles no third-party scripts, trackers, or advertising SDKs, so no
third-party cookies are set by the App. If you follow an external source link, the
destination website may use its own cookies under its own policy.

## Managing your data
You can clear all locally stored data at any time using Reset progress in the App, or
by clearing site data / uninstalling.
