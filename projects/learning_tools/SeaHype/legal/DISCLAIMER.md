## Accuracy and verification
We strive for accuracy and cite reputable public sources, but science advances and
errors are possible. Always verify critical or safety-related information against
primary sources before relying on it.

## Safety around water and wildlife
Always follow local laws, regulations, and safety guidance for any beach, water,
boating, or field activity. Never handle protected, venomous, or otherwise dangerous
marine life. When in doubt, observe from a safe distance and consult qualified local
authorities.

## Illustrations
All sea-life and shell illustrations are original vector diagrams created for the App,
not photographs. Real organisms vary in colour and form; use the linked sources and
field guides for photographic reference or precise identification.
