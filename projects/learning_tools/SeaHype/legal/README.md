# SeaHype Marine Biology Education - Legal & Compliance Pack

Version 1.0.0 - effective 5 July 2026.

These documents accompany the app for store listings, a website, and internal
records. The in-app Legal & privacy screen mirrors this content. Before public
release, the operator (or a licensed attorney) must replace every [BRACKETED]
placeholder - most importantly:

- [GOVERNING JURISDICTION] - the state/country whose law governs the Terms.
- [CONTACT EMAIL OR URL] - a real support/legal contact channel.
- [LEGAL ENTITY / OPERATOR NAME] - if a formal entity is used instead of the
  operator alias "openMarineDB".

Files
- TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md
- PRIVACY_POLICY.md
- DISCLAIMER.md
- COOKIE_POLICY.md
- OPEN_SOURCE_LICENSES.md
- SECURITY.md
- LEGAL_COMPLIANCE_AUDIT.md  (executive audit + compliance/security/doc checklists)

These templates are provided for transparency and convenience and are NOT legal
advice. Have them reviewed by a licensed attorney for your jurisdiction and
distribution channels before release.
