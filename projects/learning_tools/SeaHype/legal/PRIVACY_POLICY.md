## Summary
Your privacy is protected by design. The App has no account system, collects no
personal information, uses no analytics or advertising, and sends nothing about you
off your device. Everything you do stays on the device you use.

## Information we do not collect
We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise or approximate
location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage
analytics. We do not build a profile of you, and we do not sell or share personal
information, because we do not have it.

## Information stored only on your device
A display name you choose, your lesson progress, streaks, badges, settings, and any
notes or flags you create are saved in your browser's local storage on your device.
This information never leaves your device and is not visible to us. You can erase it
at any time with Reset progress, or by uninstalling or clearing the App's storage.

## Cookies and tracking
The App does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, web beacons, or cross-site
trackers. Local storage is used purely to remember your own progress on your own
device and is not a tracking technology. See COOKIE_POLICY.md.

## Analytics, crash reporting, and advertising
There is no third-party analytics, no crash-reporting service, and no advertising. No
behavioural data is gathered or transmitted.

## Third-party integrations and AI
The App contains no third-party trackers or SDKs that collect data and does not send
your data to any AI service. If you tap a link to an external source, your device's
browser opens that site, and that third party's own privacy practices then apply.

## Children's privacy (COPPA and similar)
The App is suitable for family and classroom use. Consistent with the Children's
Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws, the App does not knowingly
collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because nothing
is transmitted, no data-collection parental-consent step is required; a parent or
guardian should still supervise use and accept the Terms for a child.

## Your rights and controls
Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access,
correct, export, or delete on our side. You remain in full control on your device:
Reset progress erases everything immediately, and uninstalling removes all stored data.

## Data retention
Data you create is retained only on your device and only until you erase it or remove
the App. We keep no server-side records because the App has no server component for
user data.

## International users, GDPR and CCPA/CPRA
The App is designed so that the data-processing obligations of laws such as the EU/UK
GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) are inherently
satisfied: we are not a controller or business processing your personal data, we do
not transfer personal data internationally, and we do not sell or share personal
information. Your local storage remains under your control regardless of where you are.
For any statutory request, contact [CONTACT EMAIL OR URL]; we will explain that no
personal data is held.

## Security
The App runs on your device with no accounts, no logins, and no servers holding your
data, so the usual online attack surfaces (credential theft, server breaches, data
interception) do not apply to your personal data. Your information is only as
accessible as your own device; keep it secured with the platform's normal protections.
See SECURITY.md.
