1. ABOUT THIS APP
-----------------
PaleoHype Dinosaurs & Prehistoric Life ("PaleoHype," "the app") is a free,
offline educational study tool about dinosaurs, fossils, and prehistoric life.
It is published and maintained by its operator under the alias "openPaleoDB"
(referred to here as "the operator"). The app teaches introductory concepts in
paleontology, geology, deep time, and prehistoric life through bite-sized
lessons, quizzes, illustrations, and reference material.

The app runs entirely on your device. It requires no account, collects no
personal information, and stores your progress only in your own web browser's
local storage on the device you use. The operator does not receive, transmit,
or have access to your name, your progress, or any usage data. Clearing your
browser data, or using the in-app reset, will permanently erase your saved
progress.

4. IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION
-------------------------------
Fossil hunting and outdoor exploration can carry real risks. Please read and
follow these guidelines:

  * Respect all laws, regulations, and landowner permissions. Collecting
    fossils, rocks, or minerals, and entering certain lands, may be
    restricted or illegal. Many parks, preserves, and protected sites
    prohibit removing or disturbing natural and cultural materials, and some
    require a permit. Always obtain permission and follow local, state,
    tribal, and federal rules before collecting anything.

  * When in doubt, observe and photograph rather than collect, and consult a
    qualified local expert, museum, or land manager about what is permitted.

  * Practice "Leave No Trace": stay on marked trails and paths, pack out
    everything you bring in, and leave fragile sites undisturbed for others
    and for future research wherever collecting is not clearly permitted.

  * Always prioritize your own safety outdoors: tell someone your plans,
    watch the weather and terrain, wear appropriate protection when near
    rock faces or excavation sites, carry water, and supervise children.
    Minors should explore outdoors only with a responsible adult.

  * This app is not a guide for identifying, valuing, buying, or selling
    fossils, and does not provide any of that guidance.
