# Gate 1: audit of the running r6.6 app, and the remediation register

*Updated at r7.0 (2026-10-16): the last column records the status of each item and the test that proves it.*

Date: 2026-10-16. Method: every existing suite (see `01_GATE0_BASELINE.md`), plus a new runtime audit (`docs/audit/runtime_audit.js`) that boots the untouched baseline in headless Chromium at four sizes (390×800 and 360×640 phones with touch, 820×1180 tablet, 1440×900 desktop with a mouse), enrolls through the real Terms gate, visits all 9 places, 20 menu scenes and all 27 games, screenshots each, records every network request, console and page error, measures the CSS-pixel size of every text run drawn on the canvas, presses Escape out of every scene to test the way back, and reloads offline. Raw results: `docs/audit/runtime_audit_r6.6.json`; contact sheets: `docs/audit/sheet_*.png`.

## What the app is (verified at runtime)

- 58 registered scenes on one canvas (270 units wide); 9 walkable places; 27 games, all launch and draw without errors; 55 units in 4 stages, 500 lessons, 3,004 challenges; 32 Library books on 7 shelves; 34 badges; 17 fleet aircraft and 37 Hall of Flight aircraft.
- No page errors and no engine frame errors in any scene at any size. Ready in 0.35 to 0.48 s. A fresh cadet's whole footprint is about 2 KB in `localStorage` (keys `hfq_roster_v1`, `hfq_acct_<id>`, `hfq_localdb_v1`).
- Every scene has a way back: Escape from each of the 20 menu scenes returns to the airfield within one to three steps. No dead ends found.

## Recommendations (not defects)

1. Lesson and quiz screens stay canvas; a future round could add an HTML "accessible mode" renderer for the quiz engine, which would make every assessment screen-reader friendly.
2. The 446 lessons added in round 6.4 still need a fact check against FAA sources (carried over from the round 6.5 debt list).
3. A seventh academy phase can be appended above 2,600 XP once Book 7's title is set; appending does not change anyone's current phase.
4. The book series and the app use three publisher names across materials (Hype Learning Academies in the license, Hype Learning Academies on the covers, another imprint in older notes). Confirm the legal entity before release.
