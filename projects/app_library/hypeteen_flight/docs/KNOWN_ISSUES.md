# Known issues and limitations at r7.4

Truthful list. Nothing here is hidden behind a passing test.

1. **Half of the commissioned art is still soft.** The illustrator exported 22 of the 44 delivered files from 512-px previews and enlarged them (their own manifest says so): the title painting and hero, seven of nine walls, three Hub tiles, all seven floors and three discs. The raw-download ZIPs sent afterwards hold the full-size originals of the other 22 and some extra generations, but not these. They are on brief and in the app, but read soft on a phone; the round-2 request (`docs/ART_ROUND2_CORRECTIONS.pdf`) asks for them at full size, and they drop in file-for-file. Two tiles stay at their r7.1 art (rejected: lettering, a frame, wrong light colours), `badge-firstflight` and `badge-navigation` are in-house stand-ins, and the profile disc is a code-made glyph. Every decision, file by file: `docs/ART_ACCEPTANCE.md`.
2. **Canvas text size.** The HTML screens follow the text-size setting; the 58 canvas scenes still draw small text on phones (audit D07: 57% of runs under 9 CSS px at 390 px). A per-screen minimum-size pass is the next accessibility step. Every canvas screen's information is also reachable through the Hub's HTML screens, which is the screen-reader path.
3. **Games are visual.** The 27 games remain canvas-only and need sight and touch or a mouse; the Hub lists and launches them with descriptions, but there is no non-visual equivalent of playing them. They never gate progress: tiers come from lessons, checkrides and quizzes only.
4. **nginx config not validated here.** `tools/hosting/nginx-hfq.conf` and `hfq-security-headers.conf` were rewritten and the CSP they declare is tested in a browser (`qa/hfq_r7_offline.js`), but `nginx -t` was not available in this environment. Run it before deploying.
5. **Terms of Use need counsel.** Version 1.3 describes the app accurately (local storage, backups, encryption, PINs, no third-party requests, the gate before the title, the accuracy notice). It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. The contact is dustin@dbmb.io; no mailing address is given. **Parental consent:** the gate records that an adult read and accepted the Terms on this device or profile (version, time, a fingerprint of the text). It does not verify that the person is an adult or a parent, so it is not verifiable parental consent under COPPA or similar rules; the Terms say so, and whether the household arrangement meets the rules where the app is offered is a question for counsel.
6. **Content audit (D12) is open.** `qa/content_audit.js` reports that the correct multiple-choice answer is the longest option 67.3% of the time (996 of 1,480; the new HELICOPTERS unit: 0%), 257 weak distractors and 21 duplicate prompts in the older ground-school lessons. This needs an author's content pass; it was not changed by machine. The r7.4 clue ladder never shows the answer until the third miss, so the length cue is the only remaining give-away.
7. **Explorer coverage is a starter set.** 46 verified places (35 US, 8 CA, 3 MX). The app says so. Hours and access change; the data carries the date each source was checked (2026-10-16).
8. **Encrypted backups need a secure context.** WebCrypto is unavailable on plain `http://` (except localhost), so the passphrase option is hidden there with an explanation. Deploy over https.
9. **Private windows.** Some private-browsing modes refuse IndexedDB: photos and restore points switch off (the screens say so); saves still work in `localStorage` for the session.
10. **Tamper resistance.** With no server there is no trusted record: the integrity hash detects corruption, not deliberate edits, and a PIN is not authentication. Both are stated in the app and the Terms.
11. **Series interior art.** The books ship text-only by the author's decision; `[[IMG:...]]` placeholders are dropped at import and can be mapped to images later without touching the manuscripts.
12. **Device matrix.** Automated coverage ran in headless Chromium at phone (390×800, 360×640), tablet (820×1180) and desktop (1440×900) with touch, mouse and keyboard. Real iOS Safari and Android Chrome were not available in this environment; the service worker, `storage.persist()` and WebCrypto paths are standards-based but should be confirmed on real devices before announcing.
13. **Legacy scenes.** `library_r6` and `book_r6` (the canvas Library) are still registered and unreachable from the UI (`hfq_reader.js`); they can be removed in a cleanup release.
14. **Legal entity placeholders in Terms** (see 5) and the KHFQ identifier (fictional, kept) are the only intentional loose ends in user-facing text.
15. **One aircraft drawing is flagged, not fixed.** `qa/hfq_ac_audit.js` checks the 62 profiles against their published length and height (the generic floatplane and the B-2, drawn as a plan view, are skipped for height) and all pass but one. The North American T-6 Texan's published height (3.56 m) does not say what it is measured to; the drawing is 3.1 m to the propeller tip in the three-point attitude. It is reported, not altered, pending an expert check. A few helicopter figures could not be confirmed to the inch from a primary page in this environment (Bell 206 height 2.85 vs 2.91 m in other sources; the Ka-32's first-flight year, dimensions and 13-passenger figure; the MD 520N, V-22, S-64 and AH-64 dimensions come from standard references and were not re-opened). The R22 and EC135 figures are the manufacturers' own.
16. **Clues are chosen by machine.** The instructor's clue ladder (`hfq_crewtalk.js`) picks the briefing card that shares the most key words with the question and answer, and a second-clue sentence from it that does not contain the answer words. It is built only from the lesson's own checked text and never invents, but the chosen card can be the second-best one for a few questions and the second clue can be thin. A per-question author pass would make it better; the SHOW ME path always gives the answer and its why.
17. **The crew's lines are templates.** Repeat-visit lines about progress come from the academy's own records (lessons, checkrides, spotted aircraft, today's weather) and say nothing about aviation that is not already in a checked line. The stuck card suggests a station from a fixed list per room. Neither is generated text.
18. **Learn-for-a-retry has 32 lessons** across four games (`js/content/retry.js`, sources in the file). A cadet who plays a game often will see them all and the set starts again; a right answer never adds an attempt, only replaces the weakest one.
19. **Milestone cards take one tap.** A tier-3 notice (a new phase, a stage graduation) waits for a results screen or the airfield and then holds the screen until it is tapped, Escape is pressed or five seconds pass; the tap that closes it never lands on what is under it. On a results screen that means one extra tap before CONTINUE. This is deliberate; the QA drivers do the same tap.
20. **Helicopter content review.** The HELICOPTERS unit, the rewritten "How Helicopters Fly" book and the rotorcraft ID cards were written from the FAA Helicopter Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-21B), AC 91-32B and 14 CFR 61, then checked by a second pass that opened the sources; its findings were applied (Cierva's 1923 autogiro was flown by Alejandro Gomez Spencer, not Cierva; the Chinook's four landing legs carry six wheels; blade pitch is the angle the blade is set at, not the angle it meets the air; the hover's sideways drift comes from tail-rotor thrust; "does not drop like a rock" rather than "does not fall"; Part 61 named for the 40- and 150-hour minimums; "a main reason" for VNE; "descending steeply" for settling with power; "many Kamov helicopters" are coaxial; the Bell 47 seats 2 to 4; the EC135 figures are labelled as the EC135's). The pages that could not be opened are listed in item 15.
21. **The 512-lesson flight is intermittent inside `run_all`.** In the final r7.4 run it crashed once at 706 s with no failing check and passed 32/32 when rerun alone on the same build (both are in `docs/QA_RUN_ALL_r7.4.log`); the previous full run passed it. The cause was not found before packaging; `run_all` now prints a crashed suite's last lines so the next occurrence is diagnosable.
22. **r7.4.1 shipped with a partial test run.** Only the arch, r7.4 and smoke suites, plus the structure, content and byline checks, were rerun after the launch cut (no welcome page, gate-first Terms, new emblem). The other suites were updated for the new boot but not rerun; `node qa/run_all.js --dist` is the first thing to do in the next round.
