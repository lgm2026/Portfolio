# Explorer destinations: data, citations and licenses

Generated from `web/js/content/explorer/destinations.js` (46 places: United States 35, Canada 8, Mexico 3). Regenerate with `node tools/export_destinations.js`.

## Policy

- A **verified starter set**, not a complete directory of North American aviation places; the app says so on screen.
- Only places open to the public. Memorials, including the one memorial at an air-crash site, are included with care, marked, and carry no learning game or quiz. Nothing points to wreckage, restricted areas, hazardous places or private property.
- Every place carries an explicit **safety class**: `indoor`, `outdoor`, `ship`, `airfield`, `base` (on or beside a military base: entry rules apply), `memorial`, `crash-memorial`.
- Coordinates: decimal degrees, WGS 84, from each place’s Wikipedia geotag unless a source listed for the place names another (HMdb, Atlas Obscura, Mapcarta, a Wikipedia list). Checked on the date shown.
- Hours, prices and entry rules change: every page tells families to check the official website before going.
- **Media**: none shipped. The app draws its own icons; no photographs of the places are included, so no photo licenses are involved.
- **Map data**: Natural Earth 1:50m (public domain), via sane-topojson, pre-projected by `tools/build_basemap.js` (conic equal-area, parallels 20°N/60°N, central meridian 100°W).
- **Text**: summaries and histories were written for cadets from the cited sources and are the publisher’s own copy. Wikipedia is cited as a source of facts and coordinates; no Wikipedia text is reproduced.
