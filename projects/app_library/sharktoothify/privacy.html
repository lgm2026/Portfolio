Local-first by design: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no sale of data. Your collection lives on your device.

Data Stored on Your Device (never uploaded)

Profiles, find logs, tally counts, badges, report images, preferences, saved locations, purchase token, and consent status are stored in your browser's localStorage and IndexedDB on this device only. We operate no user accounts and keep no server copy of your collection. The optional Backup file you export contains this same data — treat it like the personal file it is.

Data That Leaves Your Device (only when you use these features)

(1) AI analysis: the tooth photo and any notes you submit are sent over HTTPS to the SharkToothify analysis service and processed by our AI provider (Anthropic) solely to generate your result. We do not store your photos on our servers. (2) Tides & weather: approximate coordinates or a ZIP code are sent to NOAA, the U.S. National Weather Service, and open geocoding services (Zippopotam; Nominatim, © OpenStreetMap contributors) to fetch conditions. (3) Purchases: checkout is handled entirely by Stripe under Stripe's own privacy policy — card details never touch SharkToothify. To meter analyses, our server keeps a minimal ledger: a random device identifier, a random credit token, credit counts, and timestamps. None of it is linked to your name or identity. (4) Content delivery: the app loads fonts and libraries from CDNs (Google Fonts, unpkg), which receive standard web-request data under their own policies.

Cookies & Analytics

SharkToothify sets no tracking cookies and runs no advertising or profiling. localStorage is used strictly to make the app work. If analytics are ever enabled, they will be cookieless, aggregate-only page statistics (Plausible), with no personal identifiers.

Children

SharkToothify is family-friendly, but it is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases require an adult (18+) or a parent/guardian.

Your Rights (including GDPR / CCPA)

Because your data lives on your device, you can exercise most rights yourself: view it in the app, export it via Settings → Backup, and erase it with Clear App Data or by clearing browser storage. For the minimal server-side purchase ledger, email us (include your device id or credit token from the app) to access or delete those records. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we honor applicable GDPR rights of access, rectification, erasure, and portability to the extent we hold anything at all.

Retention, International Transfers & Security

Device data persists until you delete it. Server ledger entries are kept while needed to honor purchases and prevent abuse, then deleted. Processing occurs in the United States. We protect data in transit with HTTPS, keep all API credentials server-side, never handle card numbers, and log no photo content. Report security concerns to updates@sharktoothify.us.

Changes & Contact

We will post any material changes here with a new effective date. Privacy questions and rights requests: updates@sharktoothify.us (SharktoothifyUS).
