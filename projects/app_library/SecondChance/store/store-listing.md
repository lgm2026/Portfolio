## Full Description (both stores, 4000 char limit)

Second Chance does two jobs, and it does them privately.

**Stay steady.**
Cravings peak and fade, usually within 10 to 20 minutes. You don't have to win an argument with an urge. You just have to outlast it. Second Chance gives you a plan for exactly those minutes:

• A Struggling button on every screen that opens one minute of guided breathing
• Six original arcade games to ride out the wave
• A five-minute guided workout to burn off what's left
• One-tap access to 988, SAMHSA, and your own support contacts
• Meeting finders for NA and AA

**Track what you're building.**
• Sobriety clock with milestone chips from 24 hours to 5 years
• A private diary and urge log that shows your cravings getting weaker over time
• Daily check-in with a HALT scan
• See the money you've saved
• A printable progress report for a counselor or PO

**Clear your record.**
If you've served your sentence and met your terms, you may be eligible to seal or expunge an old conviction. Second Chance explains the process in plain language:

• Relief options, waiting periods, and typical costs for all 50 states and DC
• An honest case-strength check based on your situation
• A cost estimator covering DIY, legal aid, and private attorney routes
• Step-by-step guidance for filing yourself
• Directories for free legal aid and expungement clinics

**Find something to fill the hours.**
A hobby finder that matches your interests and sorts by cost, with tips for actually sticking with it.

**Private by design.**
No account. No cloud. No tracking. No ads. Everything you enter stays on your device, and you can export or erase all of it any time.

---

Second Chance provides information and self-help tools. It is not legal advice, medical care, or an emergency service. State law information reflects law current as of early 2026; always confirm with official sources or a licensed attorney. If you are in crisis, call or text 988.

## Reviewer Notes (paste into App Store Connect "Notes for Reviewer")

Second Chance is a self-help and informational app for people in addiction recovery and people seeking to clear an old criminal record.

- No account or login is required. There is nothing to sign in to.
- The app collects no data whatsoever. All user entries are stored locally via device storage and never transmitted. This is why the privacy label declares no collection.
- All external links open independent public resources (988 Lifeline, SAMHSA, AA, NA, state legal-aid directories). We are not affiliated with any of them.
- The app displays clear disclaimers that it does not provide legal advice, medical care, or emergency services, and directs users in crisis to 988.
- Legal consent to Terms and Privacy Policy is required on first launch, with the crisis line accessible without agreeing.
- No in-app purchases, no ads, no third-party SDKs.
