Second Chance

Legal & privacy documents · Version 1.0.0

Second Chance helps people in recovery stay steady and helps people with a criminal record understand how to clear it, state by state. The app stores everything on your own device: no account, no cloud, no tracking.

If you are in crisis: This app and these documents are not an emergency service. Call 911, or call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) for free, confidential help any hour.
